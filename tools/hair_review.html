Hair review: every hair style, facial hair and head covering

Each pair shows the v2.2 drawing (left, "old") and the v2.3 drawing (right, "new") with the same face, skin tone and hair colour. The new curly and coily styles have clustered curls of varied size along the edge and coil or ringlet texture inside, instead of a ring of identical circles. Locs and braids are drawn rope by rope: locs thick and uneven with knobbly texture, braids even with a plait pattern and parted sections, over the crown from a centre part, framing the face and falling behind and in front of the shoulders. The bob is fuller and longer, with the ends curving in; "Bob" (no bangs) is new in v2.3, so it has no old version. Every other style was redrawn too: buzz cuts with grain that matches the hair type, a bald fringe that sits behind the ears, crops and side parts with strands and soft hairlines, shoulder-length and long hair ending in soft tips, stubble and beards with hair texture, a hijab with folds and a turban with layered pleats.
