Avatar style sheet

Prototype drawings for the new avatar designer: more face features, finer adjustment and soft shading. Nothing in the running experiment has changed (it is still v1.2.2). Every face on this page is drawn live by the prototype renderer, so what you see is what the experiment would show.

Round 4 · what changed and what we need

  1. Unfamiliar person: iris colour and freckles are copied again. The 3 changes are a hair style from the similar-style list (same length and texture), a different shirt colour (always), and either a hair colour change of at most 2 steps or a glasses change. Head coverings and bald heads have their own rules (section 3, special cases).
  2. Hair colours are now an ordered natural scale (black, dark brown, brown, light brown, blonde, with auburn next to brown), and grey is copied as an age cue.
  3. Foils are hand-picked: 6 style and 6 colour changes, balanced by half and gender, and every new style or colour is also worn by a stranger. Section 5 checks these rules on the page.
  4. Figures now turn three-quarters toward each other in scenes, looking at the object is a clear head tilt with eyes down, and the mouth opens 1.5× wider (sections 2 and 6).
  5. Please approve the foils (section 5) and the gaze and turn (sections 2 and 6). After that I will write the build plan.

Proposed 12 strangers and their foils

    9 · Proposal

    Designer layout, safeguards and open questions

    Designer tabs (fixed order) · 16 sliders

    Starts from the neutral face shown in section 7 (every slider at its midpoint), with default categorical options. Each tab has a "Reset section" button. There is no randomize button.

    Safeguards against caricature

    • No preset or option is linked to a skin tone or group. Every hair texture, lid type, nose and lip setting is available with every skin tone.
    • Slider ranges cover ordinary adult variation only; section 5 shows the end points. Nose width spans about ±25% around the middle and lip fullness about ±30%.
    • Eye opening is identical for every lid type, so monolid and hooded eyes are never drawn narrower.
    • Lip colour is computed from the skin (a slightly deeper, warmer shade), never a fixed red or pink.
    • Coily hair, braids and locs get the same level of detail as straight hair. Head coverings are plain, with no patterns or ornament.
    • The skin-relative contrast rule from v1.2.2 carries over: features stay equally visible on every skin tone.
    • Suggested before launch: have a few people from the communities shown review this sheet.

    Proposed config values (tunable after the pilot)

    Recorded for every participant

    • Full feature vectors for the known and unfamiliar avatars, and the distance between them.
    • Full feature vectors for each stranger and foil (fixed in the stimulus file, so every participant sees the same faces).
    • Likeness rating after the designer: "How much does this avatar look like [name]?" (1–7).
    • Time in the designer and number of edits (also per tab), plus time per section.

    Open questions (round 4)

    • For a person with long straight or long wavy hair, the similar-style list has no other long style (long ↔ shoulder length crosses lengths), so the unfamiliar person gets the nearest style of the same length, which is a bun. Allow the change to shoulder length instead?
    • Foils for s04 and s12 both change to a short crop, and s06's foil changes to a side part. Is crop ↔ side part a big enough change at scene size?
    • Do the foils (section 5) and the turn and gaze (sections 2 and 6) look right? If so, I will write the build plan for your approval.
